# Art credits

Everything in `web/public/scenes/` and `web/public/models/` was built and rendered for this project in
Blender 4.5 (Cycles) from the scripts in `art/blender/`. The character (Jingle, formerly Pip), the workshop, the door,
the lock, the wreath, the toys, the gramophone, the Star Machine, the key, the snow globe and the
ornament, the garden window ledge, the brick chimney stack, the stone viaduct, the toy train, the beacon star and
the gingerbread cookie are modelled procedurally in those scripts. The third-party assets below are all **CC0 1.0**
(public domain), so no attribution is legally required; they are listed for transparency.

## Font

The "24 12" on the back of the key is real geometry made from Blender's built-in font (Bfont, derived
from DejaVu Sans, free licence), converted to a mesh. It is the only text anywhere in the art.
